# ORA brand images

Source: [Ora logo.svg on Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Ora_logo.svg),
attributed there to Great Wall Motor and sourced from https://www.gwm.com.cn/.
The source page marks the geometric/text logo as PD-textlogo. ORA and GWM remain
their owners' trademarks; these images identify the vehicle without implying affiliation.

`ora-source.svg` preserves the downloaded source. The icon uses its emblem paths;
the logo uses its ORA wordmark paths. SVG variants adjust canvas and colour for
light/dark interfaces. PNGs are rasterisations of those SVGs. Home Assistant
2026.3+ serves these brand images locally.
